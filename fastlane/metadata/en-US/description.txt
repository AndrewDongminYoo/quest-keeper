Tired of your to-do list? Quest Keeper turns today's tasks into dungeon monsters.

The closer the deadline, the stronger the monster — but finish in time and your pixel hero takes it down in a single swing. A missed task is not a punishment; it turns into "Try again tomorrow," and yesterday's misses quietly clear so every day opens on a fresh dungeon.

Stack up small wins like a game.

■ How it works
· Add a task and the monster's strength is set by its importance and the time left until the deadline.
· Finish before the deadline and it is a one-hit victory, with pixel art to make it land.
· Missed tasks show up briefly under Today's Graves, then reset cleanly the next day.
· Try again tomorrow moves a deadline without any fuss.

■ Features
· Deadline reminders: local notifications that fire only when they matter.
· Home Screen widget: check today's dungeon and your hero without opening the app.
· Fully offline and local: no account, no login, no ads.
· No data collection: everything stays on your device.

■ Made for
· Anyone who has felt guilty about a growing backlog
· Anyone who wants procrastination to be fun to fight
· Anyone who loves retro pixel games
