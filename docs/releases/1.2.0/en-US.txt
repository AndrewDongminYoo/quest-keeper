Quest Keeper 1.2.0

• Quest Keeper now speaks English — every screen, notification, and widget follows your device language.
• Create a quest from Shortcuts or Siri without opening the app.
• Add a description to a quest, and open its detail screen from any row or notification.
• Tap a monster to find out why it looks the way it does.
• Quests whose monster grew while the app was closed now carry a marker.
• Added a way back to notification permission from inside the app after it was denied.
